How to play

        
      

      
Guess the five-letter word in six tries. Each guess has to be a real word. Hit Enter to lock it in.

Daily and practice

      
Daily is one shared word for the calendar day. Finish it — win or lose — and it stays locked until tomorrow. Your streak moves only on the daily puzzle.

      
Practice deals a fresh word whenever you want. Those games still count toward played and wins, but they do not touch the streak.
